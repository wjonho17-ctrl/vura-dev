Built around the EBM 2.1 spec

Every endpoint, every code, neatly tucked behind a calm UI.

From device initialization to stock reconciliation, VSDC Manager covers the eight EBM 2.1 service categories and turns each one into a workflow your cashiers and accountants actually enjoy.

Item & product catalog

Maintain SKUs with proper item classification, packaging unit, quantity unit and tax type — synced to the RRA item registry.

  • 10-digit RRA item classification picker
  • Tax types A / B / C / D auto-applied
  • Group prices L1–L5 for tiered customers

Sales & invoice signing

Build a basket, hit save, and the invoice comes back signed with internal data + signature data ready for the printed receipt.

  • Normal, Copy, Training & Proforma receipts
  • Cash, card, mobile money, bank transfer
  • One-click cancel / refund flows

Purchases & imports

Pull purchase data your suppliers registered against your TIN, accept it, and merge customs declarations straight into your stock.

  • Auto-fetch from /trnsPurchase/selectTrnsPurchaseSales
  • Customs declarations into items
  • Buyer-side acceptance signatures

Stock & inventory

Stock master and stock IO sequences are first-class. Adjust, transfer between branches and watch the safety quantity light up.

Customers & branches

Manage customer TINs, branch users and insurance partners (for pharmacies). Branch user accounts pushed via the EBM API.

Compliance, audited

Every signed receipt is mirrored to RRA EBM 2.1 and kept locally for 24-hour offline tolerance with full audit trail and remote audit support.

From WAR file to printed receipt

Four steps, no surprises.

Once your VSDC WAR is deployed locally and the device is initialized, the rest is workflow — not engineering.

Initialize device

Apply with RRA, deploy the WAR, then call /initializer/selectInitInfo with your TIN, branch ID and device serial. Keys are pulled from EBM and saved.

Load codes & items

Pull the standard code list, item classification, branch list and customer TINs. Register your products with their tax type and unit price.

Register sales

Build a basket. Save the sales transaction, then save the invoice — VSDC returns the receipt number, internal data and signature.

Reconcile & report

Confirm purchases registered against your TIN, adjust stock IO, push the new stock master and you're balanced for the day.
